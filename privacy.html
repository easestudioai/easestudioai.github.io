Effective October 21, 2026. EaseStudio Automation is operated by the Google account owner for private workflows.

Google Drive data

The app requests Google Drive access to upload AffiAuto MP4 video files and matching JSON metadata into folders selected by the owner. It lists files in those folders, reads metadata, and downloads uploaded files to verify their size and cryptographic hash. The OAuth scope technically grants access to Drive files beyond these folders; the AffiAuto workflow is configured to use the selected folders and known file IDs.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's computer with restricted file permissions. AffiAuto stores package names, file IDs, sizes, hashes, and delivery status in its private operational database. Video and JSON content is sent to Google Drive at the owner's request. Google Drive data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with other parties by this integration.

Control and retention

The owner can remove files in Google Drive, stop the workflow, and revoke the app's Google access in their Google Account security settings. Operational records are retained while needed to verify deliveries and recover interrupted jobs, and can be removed by the owner.
